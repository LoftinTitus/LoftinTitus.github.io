About me

Hello! I am Titus Loftin, a student in the Department of Chemical Engineering at The University of Texas at Austin! I have a strong passion for helping others, specifically those with some form of disability. I plan to use this passion after completing my education and pursue a career where I can mix my interests in biomedical research and medicine!

My research interests

  • Hemodynamics

    Studying flows, forces, and pressures in vasculature. Specifically interested in how thrombi affect those factors and vice versa.

  • Soft Tissue Mechanics

    Specifically, being able to inversely predict hetergenous material properties.

  • Regenerative Medicine

    Bioprinting mechanically sound cardiac tissues for replacements, bypasses etc. Promoting neovasculaization after injury, stroke, etc.

  • Biomaterials

    Developing new biocompatible hydrogels for wound healing and prevention.
